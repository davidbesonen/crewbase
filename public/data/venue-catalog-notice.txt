This extract includes U.S. music venues, theaters, arenas, stadiums,
amphitheaters, performing arts venues, fairgrounds, and selected concert
landmarks. Low-confidence and incomplete listings are filtered, and nearby
records with equivalent names are deduplicated. Coverage and addresses are
not guaranteed complete or current. Manual entries remain available.

Each record retains its Overture identifier and source license identifiers.

in each record. The data license does not apply to
